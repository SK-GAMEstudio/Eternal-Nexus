# Contributing & Alpha Testing

Thank you for your interest in **Eternal Nexus**! As the game is currently in an active **Alpha Testing** phase, community feedback and testing participation are essential to help us refine systems and mechanics.

## How to Get Involved

If you want to see the latest progress, follow development updates, share feedback or potentially be selected to participate in the Alpha Tests, join our official Discord server.

- **Discord Server:** [JOIN OUR COMMUNITY]

## Alpha Test Access

- Alpha Test access is currently **limited** and selection is not guaranteed.
- Keep an eye on the official Discord announcements for future testing waves and opportunities.

## Sharing Feedback & Bug Reports

If you are part of the Alpha Test or following our development updates:
- Share your thoughts on gameplay mechanics, balance, and progression.
- Report any bugs, glitches, or performance issues encountered in the **Eternal Nexus Engine**.
- Please refer to our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) if you discover a critical vulnerability.

*Follow the development. Join the community. Become part of Eternal Nexus.*
