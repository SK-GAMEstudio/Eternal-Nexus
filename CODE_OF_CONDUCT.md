# Code of Conduct — Eternal Nexus

## 1. Community & Player Respect
Our goal is to keep **Eternal Nexus** and its community platforms (Discord, GitHub) welcoming and enjoyable for everyone.
- **No Insults or Harassment:** Insults, hate speech, racism, sexism, threats, or personal attacks against other players or developers are strictly forbidden.
- **Keep PvP In-Game:** While **Eternal Nexus** features open PvP, clan wars, and rivalries outside the Spawn Island, all conflicts must stay strictly inside the game. Trash-talking or harassing players outside the game will not be tolerated.
- **No Spam or Trolling:** Do not flood chat channels, post unsolicited advertisements, or intentionally disrupt community discussions.
- **Protect Privacy:** Never share personal or private information (doxxing) about other players or team members.

## 2. Fair Play & Anti-Cheat Rules
To ensure a fair experience for all adventurers during Alpha Testing and beyond, strict fair-play rules apply:
- **No Cheating:** Using third-party software, bots, auto-clickers, speed hacks, or any unauthorized tools to gain an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited.
- **No Hacking or Reverse Engineering:** Attempting to hack, decompile, modify, or disrupt the **Eternal Nexus Engine**, game client, or official servers is strictly forbidden.
- **No Bug Exploitation:** If you find a bug, duplication glitch, or exploit, you must report it to the **SK-GAMEstudio** team. Abusing exploits to gain loot, levels, or ruin other players' experience will result in immediate sanctions.
- **No Real-Money Trading (RMT):** Buying, selling, or trading in-game items, accounts, or currency for real money is strictly prohibited.

## 3. Alpha Testing Confidentiality
- **Respect Test Rules:** If you are selected for closed Alpha Testing phases, follow any specific rules regarding sharing private test builds or leaks.
- **Constructive Feedback:** Criticism about the game is welcome, provided it remains respectful and constructive.

## 4. Enforcement & Sanctions
The **SK-GAMEstudio** moderation team enforces these rules across the game, Discord, and GitHub. Depending on the severity of the violation, consequences include:
1. **Warning:** A formal notice for minor infractions.
2. **Temporary Suspension:** Temporary mute on Discord or temporary ban from the game servers.
3. **Permanent Ban:** Immediate and permanent account termination and IP/hardware ban for cheating, hacking, severe harassment, or repeat offenses.

## 5. Reporting Violations
If you witness cheating, bug abuse, or toxic behavior, please report it directly to the **SK-GAMEstudio** staff via our official Discord server with screenshots or video proof if possible. For critical security vulnerabilities, please refer to our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).
